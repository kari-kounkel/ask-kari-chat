Who this is for

This alert program is for Ask Kari staff — the people who answer questions submitted through the Ask Kari chat widget on our websites. It is not a marketing list, and we do not text website visitors.

What you'll receive

One text each time a visitor sends a message, showing who wrote in, which site they came from, and the first part of what they said. Replying to the text posts your answer back into that visitor's chat.
